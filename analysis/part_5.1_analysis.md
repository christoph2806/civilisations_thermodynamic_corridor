# Analysis: Part 5.1 - Fermi's Paradox and the Great Filter

## Document Purpose
Extends thermodynamic predicament to universal scope; characterises Great Filter; Dyson swarm bottlenecks; thermal filter timescales.

---

## PROPOSITIONS

**P5.1: Universal Tier 1**
- Maintenance obligation, waste heat ceiling, Landauer floor apply to any civilisation
- No biology, culture, or institutions in derivation

**P5.2: Universal Tier 2**
- Competitive viability → gradient saturation; Jevons, competitive exclusion
- Override requires cooperative solution with no precedent

**P5.3: Tier 3 Contingent**
- Debt, money, governance accelerate but do not create trajectory

**P5.4: Thermal Filter Timescales**
- n = log₂(P_ceiling/P₀) doublings; at 2.3%, ~180 yr to 1% solar; at 1%, ~430 yr
- Billions of years opportunity vs hundreds of years viability = Fermi resolution

---

## THEOREMS

**T5.1: Dyson Swarm Bottlenecks**
- Luminosity bottleneck: Mercury disassembly ~57,000 yr at 700K; ~170 yr at 3000K
- Maintenance trap: δ at megastructure scale; at 1% degradation need ~3300K
- Repair-apparatus recursion: foundry has own δ; in-situ recycling limited
- Bootstrap trap: must grow to fund transition; growth consumes thermal window

**T5.2: G-HAT Null Result**
- No galaxy >85% starlight capture (Griffith et al. 2015)
- Thermodynamic reading: construction window too narrow for most

**T5.3: Filter Characterisation**
- Exists (Tier 1); dynamic not static (Tier 2); amplifiers contingent (Tier 3)
- Timescale: centuries to millennia vs galactic billions
