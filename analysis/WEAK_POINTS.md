# Weak Points & Validation Needs

## Empirical Claims Requiring Verification

1. **Garrett P ≈ λW (5.9 mW/$)**: Verify Garrett et al. 2022 methodology; check Hanley 2025 replication and pre-1970 divergence
2. **Assembly Theory at civilisational scale**: Sharma et al. 2023 validated at molecular level; extrapolation to Σ_tech unverified
3. **μ calibration (1.7–2.5)**: Source for "40–60% maintenance-equivalent activity"?
4. **Rebound estimates**: Brockway et al. 2021 (>50%); Saunders 2013 (126% avg)—verify meta-analysis scope
5. **AI energy**: Patterson, de Vries, Sevilla—training/inference estimates
6. **Biosphere Γ_bio ≪ Γ_tech**: Order-of-magnitude from Bar-On, Elhacham; robustness of inequality

## Logical Gaps

1. **A8 (Share function)**: sᵢ = ΓᵢΣᵢ/ΣΓₖΣₖ presented as physically motivated; is this unique? What alternatives satisfy monotonicity?
2. **A9 (Perturbations)**: Absolute continuity + recurrence—are civilisational shocks well-modeled as i.i.d. from μ?
3. **Boundary renormalisation**: "Materially perturb" threshold undefined
4. **β (replacement ratio)**: P7b requires ∂β/∂a_bio > 0; "Step 2" (combinatorial non-replicability) marked Open Problem 6

## Unstated Assumptions

1. **Single-planet focus**: Space expansion bottlenecks (Fermi) may be overcome by different engineering
2. **Cooperative override**: "No precedent" does not imply "impossible"
3. **T_hab**: Biological wet-bulb ~308K; effective civilisational threshold has wide range (292–298K in examples)

## Rhetorical vs Logical

1. **"Laws do not negotiate"**: Establishes tone; not a proof step
2. **Biosphere as "existence proof"**: Biosphere did not choose; landscape shaped by 3.5 Gyr evolution—institutional replication is different problem
3. **"Merely unprecedented"**: Softens conclusion; "unprecedented" could mean "never achieved" or "not yet achieved"
