## DEFINITIONS

**D5.2: Value Functional V(x₀)**
- V = sup E[∫₀^τ_K m(x,u) dt]
- Lexicographic: survive first, then maximise maintained stock-years
- Collapse dominated; "more, durably"

**D5.3: Stock Classification**
- Support: low-Γ substrate (biosphere, durable infrastructure)
- Control: sensing, computation, institutions (modifies U(x))
- Boundary: collectors, radiators (expands A)
- Reservoir: temporal batteries (manoeuvre energy)
- Burden: high-μ complexity, negative shadow value

**D5.4: General Viability Condition**
- Ḃ_req ≤ Ḃ_avail (exergy)
- Ṡ_exp ≤ Ṡ_sink,max (entropy export)
