# Analysis: Part 5.2 - The Problem in Manifold Space

## Document Purpose
Eight-parameter control surface; seven strategic orientations; 7×8 matrix; observational signatures; μ as discriminant.

---

## DEFINITIONS

**D5.1: Seven Strategic Orientations**
- S1: Pure Nostos (Γ-reduction, no A-expansion)—viable
- S2: Pure Kleos (A-expansion, no Γ-reduction)—almost certainly fail
- S3: Nostos-first sequential—viable
- S4: Kleos-first sequential—probably fail
- S5: Parallel—possibly viable, highest coordination
- S6: Apathy (aware, inactive)—non-viable
- S7: Ignorance (unaware)—non-viable, probably modal

---

## PROPOSITIONS

**P5.5**: Eight parameters: ε, A, T_hab, α (numerator); μ, δ, ξ, η_II (denominator)
**P5.6**: T_hab elasticity ≈32 (fourth-power); denominator elasticities = 1
**P5.7**: ε no-regrets across viable strategies
**P5.8**: μ-reduction discriminates viable from non-viable
**P5.9**: α = strategic reserve (fastest, insurance)
**P5.10**: S4 and S2 nearly identical in critical early phase

---

## THEOREMS

**T5.4: Strategic Matrix**
- Viable: S1, S3, S5 (all pursue μ-reduction)
- Probably fail: S2, S4
- Fail: S6, S7

**T5.5: Observational Signatures**
- S1/S3: thermodynamically quiet (invisible)
- S2/S7: brief brightening then silence
- S4: flash + partial structure then silence

**Validation**: Elasticity derivations; Earth parameter calibration
