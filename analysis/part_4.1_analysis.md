# Analysis: Part 4.1 - The Temporal Hierarchy of Constraints

## Document Purpose
Orders constraints by decision window and constraint bite; establishes which bind first/last; greenhouse vs waste heat distinction.

---

## CONSTRAINT HIERARCHY

| Constraint | Decision Window | Bite | Tier | Compliant? |
|------------|-----------------|------|------|------------|
| Greenhouse | Decades | ~Century | 1 physics + 3 source | Yes (fuel switch) |
| Ecological | Decades | Varies | Multiplier on others | Via burden-transfer |
| Financial | Decades-century | 7–15 yr cycles | 3 | Yes (architecture) |
| Evolutionary ratchet | Continuous | Continuous | 2 | No precedent |
| Waste heat ceiling | ~300 yr at 2.3% | When T→T_hab | 1 | No |

---

## PROPOSITIONS

**P4.1**: Greenhouse = composition of entropy export; waste heat = quantity
**P4.2**: Decarbonisation solves greenhouse; does not solve waste heat
**P4.3**: Financial reform removes r from Y ≥ (r+δ)W; widens corridor
**P4.4**: Ratchet operates always; override requires civilisation-scale coordination
**P4.5**: Tipping points (ice sheet, permafrost) = discontinuous kernel contraction

---

## IMPLICATIONS

- Address in order: greenhouse (urgent, tractable) → financial (reformable) → ratchet (stiff) → ceiling (fixed)
- Failure cascades downward; success buys time for next
