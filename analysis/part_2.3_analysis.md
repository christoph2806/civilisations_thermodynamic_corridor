# Analysis: Part 2.3 - The Waste Heat Ceiling

## Document Purpose
Derives Σ_max formula; establishes finite thermodynamic corridor; calibrates parameters; distinguishes waste heat from greenhouse constraint.

---

## THEOREMS

**T2.6 (Theorem T1): Finite Thermodynamic Corridor**
- Σ_max = (εσAT_hab⁴ - P_☉) / Γ
- *Proof*: P_☉ + P = εσAT⁴; at T_hab, P_max = εσAT_hab⁴ - P_☉; P ≥ (δξ/η_II)Σ → Σ_max = P_max/Γ (evaluated at μ=1 for upper bound)
- *Dependencies*: A1.3, T2.4, T1.1
- *Status*: **CENTRAL RESULT** of Part 2

**T2.7: Solar Three-Case Partition**
- Case 1: Surface solar, matched albedo → no net thermal addition (exempt)
- Case 2: Albedo mismatch → ΔP_albedo net addition
- Case 3: Beamed solar from space → net addition (like fission/fusion)

## PROPOSITIONS

**P2.6**: Ceiling is technology-independent in form; value depends on Γ (partially controllable)
**P2.7**: Greenhouse reduces ε → tightens ceiling before waste heat significant

**P2.9**: Waste heat ≠ greenhouse: former is quantity (total P); latter is composition (CO₂ etc.)
