# Proof Structure: Civilisation's Thermodynamic Corridor

## Main Thesis
Planetary civilisations face a finite thermodynamic ceiling on assembly stock Σ_max, determined by waste heat radiation limits. The default trajectory is non-viable; viable trajectories require Γ-reduction of unprecedented magnitude.
