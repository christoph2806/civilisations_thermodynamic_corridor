# Analysis: Part 3.1 - Maximum Power and the Evolutionary Ratchet

## Document Purpose
Establishes why civilisation drives toward ceiling: Lotka's maximum power principle; Garrett's stable Γ as equilibrium signature; Jevons as structural consequence; inter-sphere competition.

---

## OBSERVATIONS

**O3.1**: Lotka 1922: selection favours maximum throughput, not maximum efficiency
**O3.2**: Curzon-Ahlborn: real engines at η_CA < η_Carnot for maximum power
**O3.3**: Odum & Pinkerton 1955: biological systems ~50% Carnot for max power
**O3.4**: Garrett λ ≈ 5.9 mW/$ stable 1970–2019
**O3.5**: Hanley 2025: λ unstable pre-1970; shift at Bretton Woods collapse
**O3.6**: Brockway et al. 2021: economy-wide rebound >50%, many ~100%
**O3.7**: Saunders & Tsao 2012: lighting 100% rebound over 300 years
**O3.8**: Saunders 2013: 30 US sectors, avg rebound 126%
**O3.9**: Computation: 10¹⁰ efficiency gain, 10² total energy increase (super-Jevons)

## PROPOSITIONS

**P3.1**: Maximum power is corollary of competitive viability geometry (§4.3), not ecological axiom
**P3.2**: dP/dt = (α - δ)P; efficiency gain raises α → increases dP/dt (structural backfire)
**P3.3**: Jevons aggregate (Tier 2) holds regardless of economic system; channel (Tier 3) varies
**P3.4**: Absolute decoupling of energy from GDP at global level: never observed (Haberl et al. 2020)
**P3.5**: CO₂ can decouple via fuel switching; energy cannot under current Γ

## THEOREMS

**T3.1: Evolutionary Ratchet**
- Competitive dissipative structures saturate gradient; unused capacity unstable
- *Forward ref*: Formal derivation in §4.3
- *Status*: Tier 2; game-theoretic equilibrium

**T3.2: Burden-Transfer Mechanism**
- Biosphere displacement → technosphere must replace services at Γ_tech ≫ Γ_bio
- Net: less total assembly, more power demand
- *Dependencies*: O2.3, O2.4

**T3.3: Inter-Sphere Jevons**
- Green Revolution: efficiency gains expanded footprint, not reduced it
