### 2.1 Scope

### 2.2 Main Logical Chain Reconstructed

```
T1.1 (sink constraint) → T2.1 (maintenance floor) → T2.4 (P = Γ·Σ) → T2.6 (Σ_max)
    → T4.5 (gradient saturation) → T5.6 (default non-viable)
```

**Core thesis**: Planetary civilisations face a finite thermodynamic ceiling Σ_max. The default trajectory is non-viable; viable trajectories require Γ-reduction of unprecedented magnitude.

### 2.3 Tier Structure

- **Tier 1**: Physics (fixed)—Second Law, Stefan-Boltzmann, Landauer floor
- **Tier 2**: Evolutionary dynamics (stiff)—gradient saturation, Jevons, competitive exclusion
- **Tier 3**: Institutions (compliant)—debt, governance, monetary architecture

## 3. Findings

### 3.1 Strengths

- **Structure**: Clear axiomatic foundation, logical derivation chain, coherent tier structure
- **Physics**: Core claims (waste heat ceiling, maintenance floor) derive from standard thermodynamics
- **Empirical engagement**: Garrett, Hanley, rebound studies, Landauer experiments cited
- **Distinction**: "Merely difficult vs physically impossible" is analytically useful and consistently applied
- **Alignment**: Author's Results overviews match analysis documents; no substantive contradictions

### 3.2 Parameter Uncertainty

- **ξ**: "Least constrained parameter"; "most in need of dedicated empirical work"
- **δ**: "Stock-weighted mean" over a spectrum spanning five orders of magnitude
- **μ**: Calibrated ~1.7–2.5 from approximate maintenance shares
- **Calibration**: "Uncertainties are too wide for precise Σ_max"

The corridor is **well-defined in formula** but **not well-known in numerical values**.

### 3.3 Omitted Factors

- **Knowledge growth**: Not modeled as a state variable
- **Efficiency gains**: Modeled (via Jevons): they worsen the trajectory
- **Knowledge → coordination**: Not modeled; S7 (Ignorance) is failure mode, but knowledge enabling coordination is not a dynamic
- **Competitive landscape**: Treated as exogenous; Hanley shows attractor can shift, but the shift is not modeled endogenously

### 3.4 Urgency

- **Waste heat ceiling**: Centuries away (e.g. ~250–400 yr at 2.3% growth)
- **Decadal urgency** comes from:
  - **Greenhouse constraint**: "Decision window is decades"; carbon budget for 1.5–2 K being consumed now
  - **Viability boundary**: ~40 years of growth to reach Σ_phys* (braking boundary) at 2.3%
  - **Financial**: Minsky cycles 7–15 years

### 3.5 Space Expansion

- **Not mandatory**: Pure Nostos (S1) is viable—stabilise on planet via Γ-reduction
- **Optional**: A-expansion raises ceiling without bound; "only pathway" for unlimited growth
- **Bootstrap constraint**: Transition window narrow; construction and maintenance compete with thermal budget

### 3.6 Strategic Orientations

| Strategy | Viability | Key feature |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| S1 Pure Nostos | Viable | Γ-reduction, no A-expansion |
| S2 Pure Kleos | Almost certainly fail | A-expansion without Γ-reduction |
| S3 Nostos-first | Viable | Γ-reduction then A-expansion |
| S4 Kleos-first | Probably fail | Same as S2 in critical early phase |
| S5 Parallel | Possibly viable | Highest coordination demand |
| S6 Apathy | Non-viable | Aware but inactive |
| S7 Ignorance | Non-viable | Unaware; probably modal |

**μ-reduction** is the discriminant between viable and non-viable strategies.

## 4. Final Conclusions

### 4.1 Assessment of the Work

The work is a **serious theoretical contribution**. The physics is sound; the economics and evolutionary dynamics are within the range of plausible, empirically supported positions. The main weakness is the move from "no precedent" to "very difficult"—the "impossible" claim is correctly confined to physics.

### 4.2 Verdict by Society Type

**Static society** (fixed Γ, fixed growth rate, no endogenous institutional or knowledge change):

- The verdict holds: ceiling exists, default trajectory non-viable, viable requires Γ-reduction
- **Even then**, parameter uncertainties are large

**Dynamic society** (knowledge growth, institutional change, feedback between understanding and behaviour):

- The model's fixed-point assumptions break down
- The **outcome is open**: the trajectory is not determined by the model
- **Caveat**: Physics still bounds the outcome space (ceiling, floor, viability kernel). The question is whether a dynamic society finds a path within it—and that is not modeled.

### 4.3 One-Sentence Summary (280 chars)

> Verdict holds for a static society (with large parameter uncertainty). For a dynamic society with knowledge growth and feedback between understanding and institutions, the outcome is open; the model does not determine the trajectory.

3. Empirical rebound and AI energy estimates
